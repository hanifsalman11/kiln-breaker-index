Frequently Asked Questions (Skutt FireBox 8x6 & 14 (1,800W 120V 15A) Glass & Test Kiln)

What size breaker and wire gauge do I need for Skutt FireBox 8x6 & 14 (1,800W 120V 15A) Glass & Test Kiln (15.0A)?

Per NEC Article 424.3(B), electric kilns are continuous loads requiring a circuit breaker rated at 125% of nameplate amperage (15.0A × 1.25 = 18.8A). Install a dedicated 20-Amp 2-pole breaker wired with 12/2 AWG Copper (90°C THHN/NM-B) (NEMA 6-20R (240V) or 5-20R (120V)).

How much electricity does Skutt FireBox 8x6 & 14 (1,800W 120V 15A) Glass & Test Kiln cost per Cone 04 or Cone 6 firing?

Because kiln relays cycle on and off during the ramp-up (averaging ~58% duty cycle over an 8.5-hour firing), a 1.8 kW kiln uses approximately 8.9 kWh per Cone 6 glaze firing. At the US average of $0.16/kWh, one full firing costs just ~$1.42.

What happens if I plug a 240V Skutt FireBox 8x6 & 14 (1,800W 120V 15A) Glass & Test Kiln into a 208V commercial outlet?

Because heating element wattage varies with the square of voltage ($P = V^2 \div R$), dropping voltage by 13.3% (from 240V to 208V) cuts heating power by 24.9%—dropping your 1,800W kiln to 1,800W. It will still reach Cone 04 bisque, but will struggle or fail to reach Cone 6/10 without 208V-specific elements.
